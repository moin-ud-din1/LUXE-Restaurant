Our Story

More Than Just A Meal

Founded in 2010 by Chef Daniel Moretti, LUXE began as twelve tables and a belief that dining should feel like an occasion.

Today we still cook the same way: seasonal ingredients, patient technique and service that remembers your name.

Every plate leaves our kitchen with one goal — to make your evening the one you talk about tomorrow.

Meet The Chef

Passion On Every Plate

Chef Daniel Moretti

“Every dish begins with respect for the ingredients.”

Trained in Milan and Lyon, Daniel has spent fifteen years building menus that pair classic technique with the best produce each season offers.

His kitchen works with local farms and fishermen, and nothing is served that he would not eat himself.
